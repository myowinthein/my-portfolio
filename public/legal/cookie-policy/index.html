Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-01

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They can be used to remember information about your visit, such as preferences, or to support security features.

How We Use Cookies

This site uses cookies only in connection with the contact form's spam-protection system (Google reCAPTCHA). We do not use cookies for advertising, marketing, or cross-site tracking.

Categories of Cookies

  • Necessary: cookies set by Google reCAPTCHA (such as _GRECAPTCHA) when you interact with the contact form, to verify submissions are not automated. These are required for the contact form to function and do not require separate consent, as they are strictly necessary for a service you have requested (submitting the form).

This site does not set functional, analytics, or marketing cookies. Vercel Analytics, used to measure aggregated site traffic, does not use cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

Google sets the reCAPTCHA cookies described above. See Google's Cookie Policy for details on what Google collects and how it is used.

Your Consent and How to Withdraw It

Because the only cookies this site sets are strictly necessary to operate the contact form's spam protection, no separate consent banner is presented for them. If you do not wish to have reCAPTCHA cookies set, simply do not submit the contact form; you can still browse the rest of the Site without any cookies being set.

Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can also view, manage, and delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions specific to your browser (for example, Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge).
