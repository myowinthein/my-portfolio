Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-01

Who We Are

This site, myowin.dev, is the personal portfolio of Myo Win Thein (Martin), a Senior Backend Engineer / Technical Lead based in Bangkok, Thailand. It presents his professional background, projects, and writing, and provides a contact form for professional inquiries. Myo Win Thein is the data controller for any personal data processed through this site.

What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data:

  • Contact form data: when you submit the contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message.
  • reCAPTCHA interaction data: when you submit the contact form, Google reCAPTCHA processes technical and interaction data (such as your IP address, browser and device information, and mouse or touch behaviour) to verify you are not a bot.
  • Analytics data: Vercel Analytics collects aggregated, anonymised page-view and traffic data while you browse the site. It does not use cookies and does not collect personal data that identifies you.
  • Blog feed requests: the Writing section loads blog posts directly from a third-party feed proxy (rss2json). Loading this content causes your browser to contact that provider directly, which may log your IP address as part of its own standard server access logs.

We do not require account creation, do not process payments, and do not knowingly collect any special category data (such as health, biometric, or political data).

How We Use Your Data

  • Responding to your inquiry: we use the contact form data to reply to your message. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in responding to inquiries you have voluntarily initiated.
  • Spam and abuse prevention: we use reCAPTCHA to prevent automated or abusive form submissions. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping the site secure and free of spam.
  • Understanding site usage: we use aggregated analytics data to understand how the site is used. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) in improving the site.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We share data with the following third parties, strictly to operate the site:

  • EmailJS — delivers contact form submissions to our inbox as email. See EmailJS's Privacy Policy.
  • Google reCAPTCHA — verifies form submissions are not automated. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — hosts the site and provides privacy-focused analytics. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.
  • rss2json — proxies blog post content from Medium so it can be displayed on this site.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing purposes.

International Data Transfers

The third parties above operate infrastructure in the United States and other countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where your data is transferred outside the EEA, these providers rely on their own compliance mechanisms, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, to safeguard your data. Refer to each provider's privacy policy, linked above, for details of their specific safeguards.

Data Retention

Contact form messages are retained in our email inbox for as long as needed to handle the correspondence and are deleted when no longer needed. reCAPTCHA and analytics data are retained by Google and Vercel respectively, according to their own retention policies.

Google reCAPTCHA sets cookies (including _GRECAPTCHA) when the contact form is used, to help distinguish humans from bots. Vercel Analytics does not use cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.
